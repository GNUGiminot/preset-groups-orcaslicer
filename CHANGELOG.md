# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-27

- Groups can fill themselves by a rule: type, vendor, printer, printer model or part of the name.
- New presets are marked and have their own view.
- Check-up: presets whose base preset is gone or whose printers are all gone.
- Compare any two to four presets side by side.
- Load a group or a selection into the filament slots at once.
- Favourites (optionally on top) and notes to presets.
- Export and import of the plugin's settings.
- Warm and dark themes of the plugin's own, besides Orca's colours.
- Escape closes a dialog wherever the focus is.

## 0.1.0 — not released

First version: groups for your own filament, printer and process presets, a per-printer filter,
look-alike search with a side-by-side diff, PipSpool view, preset details with changes from the
base preset, putting a filament into a slot or selecting a printer or process from the page.
English and Russian, light and dark themes.
